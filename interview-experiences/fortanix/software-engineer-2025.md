## Interview Process

### Round 1 – HR Screening

* **Duration**: ~45–60 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Total experience and relevant experience (including leadership/managerial exposure).
* Experience with cloud platforms: AWS, Azure, GCP.
* Experience with Java, C/C++, C#.
* Experience with RESTful API integration.
* Experience working in Linux and Windows environments.
* Understanding of SDLC, Agile practices, sprint planning, execution, and project management.
* Experience with Bitbucket, Jenkins, GitHub, and modern CI/CD tools.
* Familiarity with Python, Rust, Golang, Lua.
* Overview of roles and responsibilities in previous positions.
* Programming language proficiency (Rust, Java, C++).
* Experience with containerization and orchestration: Docker, Podman, Kubernetes.
* Products worked on: APIs, backend systems, web interfaces.
* Self-rating for Docker and Kubernetes (1–10 scale).
* Current location, willingness to relocate to Bengaluru.
* Current CTC, expected CTC, notice period, competing offers.
* Reason for job change.

* **Outcome**: Shortlisted for technical interview.

---

### Round 2 – Technical Interview

* **Duration**: ~60 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion + Live Coding

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Tell me about yourself.
* Detailed discussion on project experience and past work.

**Coding Problems**

* [Number of Islands](https://leetcode.com/problems/number-of-islands/) – core logic and approach implemented correctly; about 90% completed, missed a few edge cases within the time limit.

## Outcome

* **Result**: Rejected
* **Feedback Received**: Informal / Implied via outcome
* **Reflections**: Interview expectations around DSA depth were not clearly communicated upfront. The interview process itself was professional and structured, and gave good insight into expectations for systems-focused roles.

---

## Tips for Future Candidates

* Expect strong DSA fundamentals even for systems/backend-heavy roles.
* Prepare graph problems (DFS/BFS, grids) thoroughly.
* Clarify interview expectations early — especially whether coding or system design will be the focus.
* Be ready to map real-world engineering experience to algorithmic problem-solving under time constraints.
