## Interview Process

### Round 1 – HR Screening

* **Duration**: ~20–30 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Total professional experience and relevant experience in Rust.
* Reason for job change.
* Verification against job requirements and role expectations.

* **Outcome**: Profile shortlisted; L1 Technical Interview scheduled on the same day.

---

### Round 2 – Technical Interview (L1)

* **Duration**: ~45 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion + Live Coding

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Tell me about yourself.
* Self-rating in Rust and justification.
* Number of Rust projects worked on; detailed end-to-end explanation of one project.
* How Rust eliminates the need for a garbage collector — ownership model, borrowing rules, and lifetimes.
* If Rust allows only one owner at a time, how does it support memory sharing across threads?
  * `Rc` for single-threaded and `Arc` for multi-threaded shared ownership.
* Follow-up: implement a counter that can be updated concurrently by multiple threads.

**Coding Problems**

* [Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters](https://leetcode.com/problems/longest-substring-without-repeating-characters/) – LeetCode Medium; approach, edge cases, and time/space complexity discussed; solution implemented successfully.

---

### Round 3 – Technical Interview (Client Round)

* **Duration**: ~30 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Detailed discussion on previous work experience based on resume.
* What is blockchain, why it is required, and why it is considered secure?
* How to efficiently render a very large list in React?
* Why is Rust better compared to other programming languages?
* What are the overheads or trade-offs when using Rust?
* Reason for transitioning from frontend development to Rust.

---

### Round 4 – Managerial Round

* **Duration**: ~30 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Expected joining date.
* Work-from-office policy and availability.
* High-level alignment with team and project expectations.

---

### Round 5 – HR Round

* **Duration**: ~30 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Salary expectations and negotiation.
* Documentation required for onboarding.
* General HR policies and next steps.

## Tips for Future Candidates

* Have a strong grip on Rust's ownership and concurrency model with clear explanations.
* Be prepared to explain at least one Rust project end-to-end.
* Practice LeetCode Medium problems in Rust, especially sliding window patterns.
* Expect follow-up questions that test practical understanding of thread safety and shared state.
