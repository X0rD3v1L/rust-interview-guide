## Interview Process

### Round 1 – Technical Interview

* **Duration**: ~60 minutes
* **Format**: Discussion + Live Coding (F2F)

**Questions & Topics Discussed**

* Detailed discussion on experience with Rust — projects, frameworks, and crates.
* Explain Ownership and Borrowing in Rust.
* How are constants defined in Rust? Where is memory allocated for them?
* Differences between the `Copy` and `Clone` traits.
* What are Smart Pointers? Explain each one, especially the difference between `Rc` and `Arc`.
* What is `unsafe` Rust and why is it necessary?

**Coding Problems**

* The interviewer handed me the keyboard; the coding problem was on HackerRank and had two parts:
  * **Part A** — Implement a basic key-value store with `get`, `put`, and `delete` operations.
  * **Part B** (unlocked after Part A) — Add transaction functionality with `BEGIN`, `COMMIT`, and `ROLLBACK` commands. ([Reference problem](https://leetcode.com/discuss/post/279913/bloomberg-onsite-key-value-store-with-tr-kcrv/))
  * Covered about 80% of the problem before time ran out; the interviewer was supportive throughout.
  * [My solution](https://github.com/X0rD3v1L/RusticJourney/blob/main/machine-coding-questions/src/bin/kv_store_with_transactions.rs)
